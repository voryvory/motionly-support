Account-scoped storage

On-device workout records are separated by account. Only online features use the information they need in Firebase.

Privacy by context

Local records and online features are treated differently.

GPS workout records are primarily stored on your device. Account, friends, challenges, and optional location sharing use Firebase for the information required to provide those features.

Read the privacy policy
  • Location is shared only for a period you choose
  • Hide both ends of a route on share cards
  • Keep workout files in an account-specific device area

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Who is Motionly for?

People starting to run, returning after a break, or looking for a simple way to track short runs and walks.

Is every feature free?

Motionly includes core GPS recording and features that depend on account access. Check the App Store and in-app purchase screen for current prices and eligibility.

Does a workout automatically share my location?

No. Friend location sharing stays off unless you explicitly choose a sharing period.

Can biorhythm guide health decisions?

No. It is entertainment and reference content and should not be used for medical, health, or workout-intensity decisions.

Where will today take you?

A short run or a walk still counts. Keep today’s step in Motionly.
