How it works

From route preview
to saved workout

Choose a destination, preview the route, and start GPS tracking. Saved workouts remain available in your history.

01

Preview a route

View a route for walking or running from your current location to the destination. Temperature and wind are available with a subscription or active 14-day trial.

02

Track with GPS

Record route, distance, time, steps, and estimated calories. View distance and time on screen during a workout.

03

Review saved workouts

View the date, distance, time, and steps for each saved workout.

Biorhythm

View today’s biorhythm.

Motionly calculates physical, emotional, and intellectual cycles from your birth date on the device. This is for entertainment and reference, not a scientific or medical assessment.

  • View three daily cycles

Additional features

Route preview, sharing, and storage

Preview routes, create workout share cards, and keep on-device records separated by account.

MAP

Routes for walking and running

Search for a destination and preview a route for walking or running before you start tracking.

CARD

Workout share cards

Create a card with route, distance, time, and steps. You can omit 200 m from each end of the route in the image.

LOCAL

Separate records by account

On-device workout records are separated by the signed-in account. Firebase processes information required by online features.

Data storage

On-device and online storage

GPS workout records are stored primarily on the device. Firebase processes information required for accounts, friends, challenges, and optional location sharing.

Read the privacy policy
  • Location is shared only after you choose a duration
  • Hide 200 m from each end of the route on share cards
  • Keep on-device workout files separated by account

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Who is Motionly for?

People starting to run, returning after a break, or looking for a simple way to track short runs and walks.

Is every feature free?

Motionly includes core GPS recording. Some features are available with a subscription or active 14-day trial. Check the App Store and in-app purchase screen for current prices and eligibility.

Does a workout automatically share my location?

No. Friend location sharing stays off unless you explicitly choose a sharing period.

Can biorhythm guide health decisions?

No. It is entertainment and reference content and should not be used for medical, health, or workout-intensity decisions.
